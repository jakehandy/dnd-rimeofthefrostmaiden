# Arcane Brotherhood

## Standing with party
- Active contractual ally (as of Session 6), via **Vellyenne Harpell**. The main party met a Brotherhood member directly for the first time; a one-shot cast engaged the faction earlier.

## Goals
- Recover/exploit Netherese ruins and artifacts. Per the one-shot, **Vraxil** recruited wizards to pillage a Netherese ruin suite for a special, very important artifact.
- Vellyenne Harpell's personal goal: recover a stolen family-heirloom orb from **Vraxil** and bring him in alive if possible.

## What we know
- Rumor since Session 1: a wizard from the Arcane Brotherhood is causing a stir in **Easthaven**.
- One-shot "To Kill a Wizard" (2026-03-03) details:
  - **Vraxil** — wizard who recruited **Dzaan** and three others to raid a Netherese ruin for a special artifact.
  - The artifact was found and stored inside a sentient Bag of Holding, **Johnny Bag'em**; its nature is Unknown but highly valuable to the wizards.
  - **Dzaan** was captured by the Easthaven guard; claimed the wizards did NOT kill the Easthaveners (something released from the ruins did) but wouldn't elaborate.
  - Opening the Netherese ruin released a creature that drove Easthaveners mad and spawned a worm-like horror (parallels the moon-cave "they are awake" pattern).
  - The one-shot party killed the wizard band; the artifact (in Johnny Bag'em) was lost into the tundra during the blizzard.
- Session 6: **Vellyenne Harpell** and her Dragonborn companion **Scrimwich** meet the main party (first seen unidentified in S5, saving the party from Duergar). She is hunting **Vraxil**, who stole a precious family-heirloom orb from her.
  - She is deeply knowledgeable about **Chardalyn**: volatile/explosive raw, spell-storing when refined.
  - She identified Za'mora's black crystal as the most refined Chardalyn she's seen, warded with a spell — and was visibly distressed to learn the party broke the moon-cave seal and released its horde.
  - Deal struck: she funds the party and pays **1000 gp** for the orb's safe return; a valuable magic item if Vraxil is delivered alive.
  - **Inferred, unconfirmed**: Vellyenne's stolen orb may be the same Netherese artifact from the one-shot (lost in the tundra inside Johnny Bag'em) rather than something Vraxil currently holds. Needs table confirmation.

## Known members
- **Vraxil** — recruiter/leader; furious about the missing bag/artifact (one-shot); now also wanted alive-or-located by Vellyenne for stealing her orb. Whereabouts Unknown.
- **Vellyenne Harpell** — see `data/campaign/npcs/vellyenne-harpell.md`. Contractual ally of the party as of S6.
- **Dzaan** — captured wizard (one-shot).
- Three other wizards — defeated in the one-shot.

## Leverage / threats
- Vraxil is still hunting his lost Netherese artifact, and is now also hunted by Vellyenne (and, by contract, the party).
- Their ruin-raiding keeps unleashing dangerous creatures — a recurring campaign pattern, now underscored by Vellyenne's alarm at the moon-cave release.
- The Easthaven leg of the main party's journey (for Cara's cure) overlaps this faction's turf.
- Vellyenne's funding gives the party real resources, but ties them to Brotherhood business and its risks.
