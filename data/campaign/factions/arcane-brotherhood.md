# Arcane Brotherhood

## Standing with party
- Unresolved (lead/antagonist thread). The main party has not directly engaged them; a one-shot cast has.

## Goals
- Recover/exploit Netherese ruins and artifacts. Per the one-shot, **Vraxil** recruited wizards to pillage a Netherese ruin suite for a special, very important artifact.

## What we know
- Rumor since Session 1: a wizard from the Arcane Brotherhood is causing a stir in **Easthaven**.
- One-shot "To Kill a Wizard" (2026-03-03) details:
  - **Vraxil** — wizard who recruited **Dzaan** and three others to raid a Netherese ruin for a special artifact.
  - The artifact was found and stored inside a sentient Bag of Holding, **Johnny Bag'em**; its nature is Unknown but highly valuable to the wizards.
  - **Dzaan** was captured by the Easthaven guard; claimed the wizards did NOT kill the Easthaveners (something released from the ruins did) but wouldn't elaborate.
  - Opening the Netherese ruin released a creature that drove Easthaveners mad and spawned a worm-like horror (parallels the moon-cave "they are awake" pattern).
  - The one-shot party killed the wizard band; the artifact (in Johnny Bag'em) was lost into the tundra during the blizzard.

## Known members
- **Vraxil** — recruiter/leader; furious about the missing bag/artifact; whereabouts Unknown.
- **Dzaan** — captured wizard.
- Three other wizards — defeated in the one-shot.

## Leverage / threats
- Vraxil is still hunting his lost Netherese artifact.
- Their ruin-raiding keeps unleashing dangerous creatures — a recurring campaign pattern.
- The Easthaven leg of the main party's journey (for Cara's cure) overlaps this faction's turf.
