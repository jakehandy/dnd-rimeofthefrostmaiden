# Duergar

## Standing with party
- Hostile.

## Goals
- Unknown in full, but conducting escalating raids across the Ten-Towns; apparently after **Chardalyn**.

## What we know
- Speaker Nimsy Huddle reports a pattern of **Duergar attacks** across the Ten-Towns, growing bolder in the endless dark — Lonelywood's devastation was unprecedented in scale (S4).
- Some Ten-Towners believe the **Duergar are behind the darkness and everlasting rime** itself (rumor, unconfirmed; S4).
- A piece of **Chardalyn** (rare pitch-black mineral) was found in the Lonelywood chapel cellar, implying a former large stash; **Duergar tracks** were nearby (S4).
- The party tracked Duergar toward **Termalaine** and, with the town prepared, **repelled a Duergar attack** there (S5).

## Known encounters
- Lonelywood attack (offscreen; villagers described "ghosts") — likely Duergar (S4).
- Termalaine attack — repelled by the party and town (S5).
- Four Duergar disintegrated by **Vellyenne Harpell** (Arcane Brotherhood wizard, identity resolved S6) during the Termalaine fight (S5).

## Leverage / threats
- The missing Lonelywood Chardalyn stash — what are they building or powering?
- Possible connection to the broader Auril / endless-rime mystery.
- Likely to keep striking Ten-Towns; Termalaine and others remain at risk.
