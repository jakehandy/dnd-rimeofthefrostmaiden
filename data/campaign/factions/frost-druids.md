# Frost Druids (Lady Ravasin's Order)

## Standing with party
- Hostile / antagonistic.

## Goals
- Court **Auril's** favor and convert the Ten-Towners to worship her (per captured-badger intel, S1).
- Continue ritual practices like Ravasin's, including the rite that cursed Cara.
- Believe disasters befalling the Ten-Towns are punishment for lacking faith in Auril, and preach repentance (S4).

## Known members
- **Lady Ravasin** — leader; the "lady with the owl face"; **killed by Tar** (S2).
- Two unnamed **Frost Druids** — proselytized at the Lonelywood chapel and showed pointed interest in Cara (S4). They speak a shared Druidic language.
- One unnamed **Frost Druid** — author of a letter recovered from a bandit ambushed on the Targos road (S6); ordered the bandits to harass the party and "bring us the girl" (presumed Cara). Not encountered in person.

## What we know / notes
- Per the captured badger, **Auril has forsaken** them — yet they persist in trying to win her back.
- They recognized Cara as the girl Ravasin used in her ritual and are interested in why Tar has her.
- Their attire is distinctive (white robes; Ravasin wore an owl mask).
- S6 escalation: a Frost Druid directly hired/ordered bandits to attack the party ("those mongrels from out of town are becoming a nuisance") and demanded Cara be brought to them — moving from passive interest to active aggression.
- The same letter indicates the Druids are moving more **Chardalyn** to other Ten-Towns — the first direct link between the Frost Druids and the Chardalyn trade previously associated with the Duergar. Whether the two factions are cooperating, share a supplier, or are unrelated Chardalyn users is Unknown.

## Leverage / threats
- They are actively interested in **Cara** and, as of S6, are directing armed attacks to recover her.
- They exploit Ten-Towns disasters for recruitment.
- They appear to control or have access to a Chardalyn supply chain reaching multiple Ten-Towns.
- Their full size, leadership after Ravasin, and connection to the Duergar attacks are Unknown.
