# Frost Druids (Lady Ravasin's Order)

## Standing with party
- Hostile / antagonistic.

## Goals
- Court **Auril's** favor and convert the Ten-Towners to worship her (per captured-badger intel, S1).
- Continue ritual practices like Ravasin's, including the rite that cursed Cara.
- Believe disasters befalling the Ten-Towns are punishment for lacking faith in Auril, and preach repentance (S4).

## Known members
- **Lady Ravasin** — leader; the "lady with the owl face"; **killed by Tar** (S2).
- **Lady Yshma** — a prominent frost druid of the **Church of the Frostmaiden**; **hired a mercenary team to abduct Cara** (revealed S8). See `data/campaign/npcs/lady-yshma.md`.
- Two unnamed **Frost Druids** — proselytized at the Lonelywood chapel and showed pointed interest in Cara (S4). They speak a shared Druidic language.

## What we know / notes
- Per the captured badger, **Auril has forsaken** them — yet they persist in trying to win her back.
- They recognized Cara as the girl Ravasin used in her ritual and are interested in why Tar has her.
- Their attire is distinctive (white robes; Ravasin wore an owl mask).

## What we know / notes (cont.)
- Now explicitly identified with the **Church of the Frostmaiden** (S8).
- A frost-druid letter recovered from a bandit ambush (S7) ordered *"...bring us the girl"* and indicated the druids are **moving more Chardalyn to other towns**.
- They escalated from proselytizing to **hiring mercenaries to abduct Cara** (S8, via Lady Yshma) — that attempt was foiled and Cara recovered.

## Leverage / threats
- They are actively interested in **Cara** and keep trying to take her (letter S7; abduction S8).
- They exploit Ten-Towns disasters for recruitment and are distributing Chardalyn across towns.
- Possible overlap with **Speaker Naerth of Targos**, who sacrifices to Auril (connection unconfirmed).
- Their full size, and leadership structure after Ravasin (is **Yshma** the new leader?), remain Unknown.
