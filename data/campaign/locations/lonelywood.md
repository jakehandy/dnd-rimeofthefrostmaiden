# Lonelywood

## Snapshot
- Region: Ten-Towns, Icewind Dale (on Maer Dualdon)
- Faction control: Ten-Towns; Speaker **Nimsy Huddle**; Frost Druids proselytizing post-attack
- Danger level: High (recently devastated)

## What we know
- Secluded Ten-Town where the campaign opened; distrustful of magic/wizardry (S1).
- Starting hub: Hadrian arrived seeking a friend; Za'mora was hired here to find the kidnapped daughter (Cara) (S1).
- Near the moon-phase ritual clearing and the "moon cave" (Netherese building) outside town.
- By Session 4, Lonelywood was **ravaged and nearly destroyed** — many dead, most buildings wrecked — by an attack the villagers called "**ghosts**." Only the **chapel** survived intact.
- The chapel cellar held a piece of **Chardalyn** (and signs of a former large stash); **Duergar tracks** were nearby (S4).

## Opportunities
- Friendly Speaker (Nimsy Huddle) as an ally and source.
- Potential rebuilding/defense quests.

## Risks
- Repeat or follow-up Duergar attacks.
- Frost Druids using the disaster to recruit/convert and to pursue Cara.
- The missing Chardalyn stash in hostile hands.
