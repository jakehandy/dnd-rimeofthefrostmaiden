# Lonelywood

## Snapshot
- Region: Ten Towns, Icewind Dale
- Faction control: Town governance under Speaker `Nimsy Huddle`; active Auril proselytizing by Frost Druids at the chapel; Duergar activity strongly suspected
- Danger level: High (recently ravaged; ongoing Duergar threat; competing factional messaging)

## What we know
- Starting hub for Sessions 1-2; party departed for the moon cave from here.
- By Session 4 the town is found "ravaged and nearly destroyed": many dead, most buildings heavily damaged or destroyed.
- Villagers report being attacked by "ghosts."
- The chapel is conspicuously undamaged and is being used as a triage center.
- Two Frost Druids in Lady Ravasin-style attire are preaching at the chapel for repentance to Auril.
- Speaker `Nimsy Huddle` is coordinating wounded care and shares Duergar-pattern intel.
- Hadrian finds a `Chardalyn` fragment in the chapel cellar and concludes a large stash was previously kept there; Duergar tracks are visible near the cellar entrance.

## Opportunities
- Nimsy Huddle as a civic ally and Duergar-pattern intel source.
- Triage/aid efforts at the chapel as a way to build local trust.
- Chardalyn cellar as a lead on Duergar logistics and possibly a destination they want to revisit.

## Risks
- Frost Druids in town may relay Cara's location and Tar's involvement to Lady Ravasin's order.
- Civilian morale is collapsing under the "ghosts" narrative + Auril preaching.
- Further Duergar attacks plausible given the unprecedented scale of this strike.
