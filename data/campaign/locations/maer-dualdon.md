# Maer Dualdon

## Snapshot
- Region: Ten Towns, Icewind Dale - large lake bordering Lonelywood and Termalaine
- Faction control: None confirmed; frozen surface usable as travel shortcut
- Danger level: High (awakened plesiosaurus encountered on the ice)

## What we know
- Frozen during the perpetual rime; party crossed it in Session 4 to try to beat Duergar tracks to Termalaine.
- Hosts at least one awakened plesiosaurus that surfaces through the ice; identified in dialogue as Lady Ravasin's "daughter."
- The plesiosaurus targeted Tar after Arjay revealed Tar killed Lady Ravasin.
- Cara entered a magical state on the ice and drove the plesiosaurus off.

## Opportunities
- Shortcut between Lonelywood and Termalaine when the ice holds.
- Possible link back to Lady Ravasin's broader awakened-creature network if the plesiosaurus is investigated further.

## Risks
- Awakened plesiosaurus is hostile to Tar specifically and may return.
- Ice surface combat - falling through is a likely hazard if the ice is damaged.
- Any further "children of Ravasin" reveal could compound the threat profile.
