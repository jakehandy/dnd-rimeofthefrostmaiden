# The "Moon Cave" (Netherese Building)

## Snapshot
- Region: Wilderness outside Lonelywood, Icewind Dale
- Faction control: Formerly Lady Ravasin / Frost Druids; now unsealed and overrun by released creatures
- Danger level: Very high (broken seal; psychic monstrosities loose)

## What we know
- Not actually a cave: an **ancient Netherese building** encased in ice after long disuse (S2).
- Hadrian confirmed its Netherese origin; wall carvings depict floating sky-cities and wizards shaping the world below (S2).
- Contains a **silver gauntlet** that controls the building's defenses (the "moon cave" of the White Moose); Uriel wore it to advance, straining it (S2).
- Held a door inlaid with a **black crystal** — a Netherese **warding artifact** (Hadrian, S3) — that Ravasin warned "holds back something terrible."
- Za'mora removed the black crystal (S2), unsealing the door; **one-eyed psychic monstrosities** poured out and climbed the stairs (S3).
- The building's stairs have lethal protections (many creatures died climbing); Arjay collapsed the stairwell roof to contain most of the horde (S3).

## Opportunities
- Likely tied to the **Ythryn** mystery ("the seal is broken; they are awake").
- Netherese lore, magic, and possibly more artifacts remain inside.

## Risks
- The seal is broken; containment (collapsed stairwell) is temporary.
- The released psychic creatures are "more than they seem" and dangerous to enter mentally.
