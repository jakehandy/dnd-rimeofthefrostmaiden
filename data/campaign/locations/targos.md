# Targos

## Snapshot
- Region: Ten-Towns, Icewind Dale (on Maer Dualdon)
- Faction control: Ten-Towns; new Speaker **Naerth** (Auril-aligned)
- Danger level: Elevated (Auril sacrifices; kobold-infested mines; deep Chardalyn vein)

## What we know
- The party travels here after rescuing Cara (S8), following the mines lead first heard from Masthew (S5).
- Recent **change of Speakership**: **Naerth** now leads and is offering **human sacrifices to Auril**, to the citizens' dismay (S8).
- The **Targos mines** are held by **kobolds** driving out miners; rumor of a **dragon** within (S5, S8).
- Interrogated kobold **Grek** reveals the kobolds are shielding Targos from a **vein of Chardalyn** deeper in the mines; their "Dragon Lord" is the kobold **Snowtoe the Coldbold** (S8).

## Opportunities
- Ally the kobolds (shared interest in containing the Chardalyn) via Grek / Snowtoe.
- Rally discontented citizens against Naerth's sacrifices.
- Advance the campaign's Chardalyn and Auril threads.

## Risks
- Speaker Naerth's Auril worship / human sacrifices; possible frost-druid overlap.
- The **volatile Chardalyn vein** deep in the mines.
- The frost druids (Lady Yshma) are moving Chardalyn between towns and hunting Cara.
