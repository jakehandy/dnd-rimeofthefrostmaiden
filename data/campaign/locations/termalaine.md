# Termalaine

## Snapshot
- Region: Ten-Towns, Icewind Dale (on Maer Dualdon)
- Faction control: Ten-Towns; Speaker **Masthew** (half-orc)
- Danger level: Elevated (Duergar pressure; just defended)

## What we know
- Reached by the party in Session 5 via a crossing of the frozen Maer Dualdon, en route to Easthaven.
- Speaker **Masthew** holds court at the **Northern Lights Inn**; he was discussing the **Targos** mines being overrun by **Kobolds** (S5).
- Warned by Arjay, Masthew helped prepare the town; the party **repelled a Duergar attack** before Termalaine took Lonelywood-level damage (S5).
- Has shops: a shoddy herbalist (homemade potion of fire breathing) and a shopkeeper who unknowingly sold magical arrows (S5).

## Opportunities
- Allied Speaker (Masthew) and a defended, friendly base.
- Markets with under-valued magic items.
- Springboard toward Easthaven (for Cara's cure) and the Targos kobold lead.

## Risks
- Continued Duergar offensive in the region.
- The mysterious woman + Dragonborn who intervened here remain unidentified.
