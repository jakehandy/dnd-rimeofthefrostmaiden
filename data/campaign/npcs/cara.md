# Cara

## Snapshot
- Role: Rescued ritual subject; the party's traveling charge
- Affiliation: Daughter of a Lonelywood woman (Cara's mother); none formal
- First seen: Session 1 (as the supposedly kidnapped daughter); recovered alive Session 2-3; named Session 4
- Current status: Alive but cursed — stabilized, not cured; failing over time; developing strange power

## What we know
- She was the "kidnapped daughter" the frantic mother hired Za'mora to find (S1).
- She was the subject of Lady Ravasin's ritual, which was cut short, leaving her in a liminal near-death state (S2-S3).
- Arjay carried her body from the moon cave; en route he realized she was still barely alive (S3).
- Tar stabilized her with his own ritual at great personal cost, but cannot fully reverse Ravasin's ritual (S3).
- She woke and named herself **Cara** (S4); returned briefly to her mother in Lonelywood.
- Frost Druids recognized her as Ravasin's ritual girl and are very interested in her (S4).
- Crossing Maer Dualdon, she entered a magical state, drove off an awakened plesiosaur, stabilized a dying Tar, then collapsed — afterward a **white streak** appeared in her hair (S4).
- In Termalaine a herbalist's homemade potion of fire breathing forced her awake (S5).

## Relationship to party
- The party feels responsible for her and is escorting her toward a cure in Easthaven.
- She has twice been a lifeline for Tar (drove off the plesiosaur, stabilized him).

## Hooks and leverage
- Only a full reversal (Druid Creuse Pelleteuse, Easthaven) is believed able to cure her.
- Her emergent power and white streak suggest a transformation/arc — nature Unknown.
- The Frost Druids want her; she is a target as well as a charge.
