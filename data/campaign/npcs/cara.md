# Cara

## Snapshot
- Role: Surviving subject of Lady Ravasin's interrupted ritual; emerging unknown magic-user
- Affiliation: None confirmed; mother is a Lonelywood resident
- First seen: Session 3 (carried out of the moon cave by Arjay)
- Current status: Unconscious after collapsing on Maer Dualdon at end of Session 4; alive but in continuing decline without ritual reversal

## What we know
- The "frantic mother" from Session 1 is Cara's mother; she had hired Za'mora to find her.
- Subject of Lady Ravasin's ritual; the ritual was cut short, leaving her in a liminal near-death state.
- Tar stabilized her in Session 3 but explicitly could not fully reverse the ritual.
- Wakes briefly on the road in Session 4 and names herself.
- The two Frost Druids at the Lonelywood chapel recognize her in Druidic as the girl Lady Ravasin was using for her ritual.
- On Maer Dualdon she enters a magical state: drives off the awakened plesiosaurus, stabilizes Tar, then collapses; a white streak forms in her hair.

## Relationship to party
- Currently traveling with the party as a vulnerable rescue and active patient.
- Za'mora was directly compensated (`horn of blasting`) for trying to save her.
- Tar carries the ongoing healer/ritual-reversal burden.

## Hooks and leverage
- Without a full reversal of the ritual she will eventually die (Tar's warning).
- Easthaven Druid `Creuse Pelleteuse` is the named lead for attempting a full reversal.
- The Frost Druids' interest in her marks her as a high-value target for Lady Ravasin's order.
- Her emerging magical state and hair change suggest something inherited or imprinted from the ritual.
