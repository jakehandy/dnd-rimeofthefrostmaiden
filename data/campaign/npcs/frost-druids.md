# Frost Druids (Lonelywood chapel pair)

## Snapshot
- Role: Two Auril-aligned Druids preaching at the Lonelywood chapel after the attack
- Affiliation: Lady Ravasin's order (same/similar attire); Auril
- Label: "Frost Druids" is the working in-world label per DM (confirmed 2026-05-19)
- First seen: Session 4, at the Lonelywood chapel
- Current status: Active in Lonelywood; last seen pressing Auril repentance on the wounded crowd

## What we know
- Both wear attire similar to Lady Ravasin's, marking them as part of (or aligned with) her order.
- They preach that the attack reflects the town's lack of faith in Auril and that everyone must repent and turn to her to regain favor.
- They show pointed interest in Cara and question Tar about how she came to be in his company.
- Speaking Druidic between themselves, they note Cara appears to be the girl Lady Ravasin was using for her ritual.
- Tar was openly disgusted by their messaging and confronted them.

## Relationship to party
- Antagonistic / suspect: actively recruiting for Auril, in the same order whose leader the party killed, and clearly aware of Cara's significance.
- Tar is the in-play counter-voice against their preaching.

## Hooks and leverage
- They likely report back to Lady Ravasin's order about Tar holding Cara.
- Whether they know Lady Ravasin is dead is Unknown.
- Pressure point: their narrative competes directly with the Duergar-attack reality Nimsy describes.
- Names and individual stat profiles are Unknown.
