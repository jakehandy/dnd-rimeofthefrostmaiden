# Grek

## Snapshot
- Role: Kobold of the Targos mines
- Affiliation: The Targos mine kobolds (led by "Dragon Lord" Snowtoe the Coldbold)
- First seen: Session 8
- Current status: Alive; cooperative after interrogation

## What we know
- A kobold in the **Targos mines**; interrogated by Arjay (S8).
- Reveals the kobolds are **not simply raiding** — they are trying to **protect the people of Targos from a vein of Chardalyn** found deeper in the mines (S8).
- **Leads the party to the kobolds' "Dragon Lord," Snowtoe the Coldbold** (S8).

## Relationship to party
- Turned cooperative; acted as the party's guide to Snowtoe.

## Hooks and leverage
- Key witness reframing the "kobold menace" as a protective effort against the Chardalyn.
