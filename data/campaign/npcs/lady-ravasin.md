# Lady Ravasin

> Spelling: player notes use **Ravasin**; earlier repo files use **Ravisin**. Same character.

## Snapshot
- Role: Frost Druid leader; "the lady with the owl face"
- Affiliation: Frost Druids / her own order courting Auril
- First seen: Named/identified Session 1; encountered in person Session 2
- Current status: **Dead** — killed by Tar in the moon cave (S2)

## What we know
- Described by Tar's snow fox as the deceptive "lady with the owl face": "She deceives. She coerces. She grants unwanted gifts. She is not an owl." (S1)
- Led ritual killings near Lonelywood — moon-phase corpse sacrifices — and commanded awakened animals (the White Moose, badgers). (S1)
- She and her order sought to court Auril's favor and convert Ten-Towners; per a captured badger, Auril has forsaken them. (S1)
- Encountered in the moon cave in white robes and an owl mask; warned the party not to open the black-crystal door "for it holds back something terrible." (S2)
- Killed by Tar (enraged at her harm to local animals), over Arjay's objection that she knew more about Ythryn. (S2)
- Mother of the awakened plesiosaur that later attacked the party (S4) — and the ritualist who cursed Cara.

## Relationship to party
- Primary antagonist of the opening arc; now dead, but her order and "children" persist.

## Hooks and leverage
- Killing her cost the party her knowledge of Ythryn (Arjay's grievance).
- Her order (Frost Druids) continues her work and hunts Cara.
- Her interrupted ritual is what is killing Cara.
