# Lady Yshma

## Snapshot
- Role: Prominent frost druid; the party's active antagonist regarding Cara
- Affiliation: **Frost Druids** / **Church of the Frostmaiden** (Auril's order)
- First seen: Named in Session 8 (not yet seen in person)
- Current status: At large; actively trying to seize Cara

## What we know
- A **prominent frost druid** in the Church of the Frostmaiden (S8).
- **Hired the mercenary team** — including the red-bearded, eyepatched kidnapper — to abduct **Cara** and bring her to the druids (revealed under interrogation, S8).
- Likely tied to the frost-druid letter from Session 7 (*"...bring us the girl"*), part of the order's ongoing effort to take Cara.

## Relationship to party
- Antagonistic. She is the apparent driver behind the attempts to capture Cara.

## Hooks and leverage
- Possibly the frost druids' leader (or a leader) following Lady Ravasin's death.
- Connects the abduction attempts, the Session 7 letter, and the druids moving Chardalyn between towns.
- The party may seek her out to end the threat to Cara — or she may strike again.
