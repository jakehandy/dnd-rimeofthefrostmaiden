# Masthew

## Snapshot
- Role: Speaker of Termalaine
- Affiliation: Termalaine / Ten-Towns leadership
- Species: Half-orc
- First seen: Session 5
- Current status: Alive; allied with the party to defend Termalaine

## What we know
- Found at the **Northern Lights Inn** discussing with the barkeep that the mines in **Targos** are overrun by a band of **Kobolds** (S5).
- Warned by Arjay of the incoming Duergar attack; together they planned to ready Termalaine for battle.
- The town's preparation helped repel the Duergar attack before Termalaine suffered Lonelywood-level damage.
- Held a **ceremony of thanks** for the party's role in defending Termalaine (S6); townsfolk gave the party offerings originally meant for Auril, which upset some of them.

## Relationship to party
- Cooperative ally; acted on Arjay's warning and coordinated the town defense.
- Publicly honored the party afterward, though the ceremony created some friction with townsfolk over redirected Auril offerings.

## Hooks and leverage
- Source of the new **Targos kobold** lead.
- A friendly Speaker who could supply aid, information, or shelter going forward.
- The ceremony's fallout (townsfolk upset about Auril's offerings) could affect the party's standing in Termalaine going forward.
