# Michael

## Snapshot
- Role: Uriel's celestial patron/contact; linked to the Morninglord
- Affiliation: The Morninglord (Lathander)
- First seen: Session 1 (vision); recurring in Uriel's dreams
- Current status: Active as a recurring vision-giver

## What we know
- Gave Uriel his founding charge (S1): find out for the Morninglord what has happened to **Auril**, and guide/protect a being completing a sacred quest.
- Appeared again in Uriel's sleep (S4) asking for an update on Auril.
- Seemed **disturbed** when Uriel mentioned the creatures released from the Netherese moon-cave building (S4) — implying those creatures matter to the larger Auril/divine situation.

## Relationship to party
- Reaches the party only through Uriel's visions; effectively their divine quest-giver.

## Hooks and leverage
- His reaction ties the unsealed Netherese creatures to the Auril mystery.
- Unknown whether "the being on a sacred quest" is Uriel himself, Cara, or someone else.
