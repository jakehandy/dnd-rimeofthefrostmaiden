# Naerth

## Snapshot
- Role: Speaker of Targos (newly installed)
- Affiliation: Targos leadership; Auril-aligned
- First seen: Session 8
- Current status: In power in Targos; unpopular with citizens

## What we know
- Came to power in a **recent change of Speakership** in Targos (S8).
- Is offering **human sacrifices to Auril**, to the **dismay of the citizens** (S8).

## Relationship to party
- Undetermined but troubling; his Auril worship and human sacrifices put him at odds with the party's sympathies.

## Hooks and leverage
- Potential flashpoint: expose, oppose, or depose him over the sacrifices.
- His rise and Auril-alignment may connect to the frost druids / Church of the Frostmaiden pressure across Ten-Towns.
