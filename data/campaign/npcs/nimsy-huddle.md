# Nimsy Huddle

## Snapshot
- Role: Speaker of Lonelywood
- Affiliation: Lonelywood / Ten-Towns leadership
- First seen: Session 4
- Current status: Alive; leading Lonelywood through the aftermath of the "ghost" attack

## What we know
- Found at the intact chapel tending the wounded after Lonelywood was devastated (S4).
- After Arjay helped her stitch a wounded man, she confided in him.
- Believes the attack resembles other **Duergar** attacks across the Ten-Towns, but at unprecedented scale.
- Fears the Duergar are growing bolder in the endless dark; notes some believe the Duergar are behind the darkness and everlasting rime itself.

## Relationship to party
- Trusts Arjay after he assisted with the wounded; a potential Ten-Towns ally/source.

## Hooks and leverage
- Key informant on the Duergar threat and Ten-Towns politics.
- Lonelywood needs rebuilding and defense — possible future quest hub.
