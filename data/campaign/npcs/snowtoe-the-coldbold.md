# Snowtoe the Coldbold

## Snapshot
- Role: "Dragon Lord" of the Targos-mine kobolds
- Affiliation: The Targos mine kobolds
- Species: Kobold (white, winged)
- First seen: Session 8
- Current status: Alive; leader of the mine kobolds

## What we know
- The kobolds' feared "Dragon Lord" and source of the "dragon in the mines" rumor is, in fact, **a kobold** himself (S8).
- Appearance: a **white, winged kobold** bearing a **golden staff shaped like a snake** (S8).
- Grek brings the party to him after they learn the kobolds are guarding against the deep **Chardalyn vein** (S8).

## Relationship to party
- Newly met; disposition still forming. The kobolds' motive (protecting Targos from the Chardalyn) suggests possible common ground.

## Hooks and leverage
- Potential ally against the Chardalyn threat in the mines.
- The "dragon" rumor is (at least partly) explained by Snowtoe — but whether a real dragon is also present is unconfirmed.
