# Vellyenne Harpell

## Snapshot
- Role: Wizard of the Arcane Brotherhood; quest-giver / potential ally
- Affiliation: Arcane Brotherhood; Harpell family
- Species: Human (presumed); a "strangely attractive" wizard
- First seen: Session 5 (unidentified, Termalaine); named and met in Session 7
- Current status: At large; in a bargain with the party
- Companion: **Scrimwich**, her Dragonborn companion

## What we know
- She is the "mysterious, strikingly attractive woman" who, with a hooded Dragonborn, **disintegrated four Duergar** to save the party in Termalaine (S5).
- In Session 7 she introduces herself as **Vellyenne Harpell** of the Arcane Brotherhood, traveling with **Scrimwich**.
- She is hunting a fellow Brotherhood member, **Vraxil**, who stole a precious family heirloom — a **mysterious orb**.
- She explains **Chardalyn**: **volatile/explosive** in raw form, **spell-storing** when refined.
- She identifies Za'mora's **Black Crystal** as the **most refined Chardalyn she has ever seen**, infused with a **warding spell**.
- She is distressed to learn the party (Za'mora) unleashed the horde of monsters from the Netherese dungeon (the moon-cave seal, S2).

## The deal (S7)
- Return her **orb** → **1,000 gp**.
- Also bring **Vraxil to her alive** → a **valuable magical item**.

## Relationship to party
- Cooperative but self-interested; twice helpful (saved them in S5, offered a lucrative contract in S7).
- Her Brotherhood ties make her allegiance worth watching.

## Hooks and leverage
- Ties directly into the Arcane Brotherhood / Vraxil / Netherese-artifact threads (see the "To Kill a Wizard" one-shot).
- Best current source of Chardalyn lore.
- May want the Black Crystal; her reaction to the unsealed dungeon is a possible future friction point.
