# Vellyenne Harpell

> Identity resolution: this is the "mysterious, strikingly attractive woman" who disintegrated four Duergar to save the party in Termalaine (Session 5). See `data/campaign/npcs/mysterious-woman-and-dragonborn.md` for that original entry.

## Snapshot
- Role: Wizard of the Arcane Brotherhood
- Affiliation: Arcane Brotherhood
- First seen: Session 5 (Termalaine, unidentified); named and spoken to directly in Session 6
- Current status: Allied by contract; funding the party to recover her stolen orb

## What we know
- Demonstrated major magical power in Session 5: disintegrated four Duergar in one action to save the party.
- Travels with a Dragonborn companion, **Scrimwich**.
- Is searching for **Vraxil**, another Arcane Brotherhood wizard, who stole a precious family heirloom of hers — a mysterious orb.
- Knowledgeable about **Chardalyn**: explains it is volatile/explosive in raw form, and capable of storing spells once refined.
- Recognized Za'mora's black crystal as the most refined piece of Chardalyn she has ever seen, infused with a warding spell — implying deep expertise with the material.
- Reacted with visible distress on learning the party (via Za'mora) released the horde of monstrosities sealed in the Netherese moon-cave building — suggesting she understands the stakes of that seal better than the party does.
- Spelling note: recap text alternates `Vellyenne` (first mention) and `Vellyene` (later mentions); `Vellyenne Harpell` is treated as canonical here.

## Relationship to party
- Contractual ally as of Session 6: funds the party and will pay **1000 gp** for the safe return of her orb; a valuable magic item if Vraxil is delivered alive.
- Motive beyond the orb (why she intervened in Session 5, before any deal existed) is still Unknown — the Termalaine rescue predates their working relationship.

## Hooks and leverage
- Her reaction to the moon-cave seal breaking suggests she may know more about the Ythryn/Netherese threat than she's said.
- Family ties to Vraxil (if any) are unconfirmed — the recap only establishes they're both Brotherhood members and that the stolen orb is "a family heirloom" of hers.
- **Inferred, unconfirmed**: her missing orb may be the same Netherese artifact from the "To Kill a Wizard" one-shot, which Vraxil sent a wizard team to recover and which was last known lost in the tundra inside the sentient bag Johnny Bag'em. Needs table confirmation.
- Her deep Chardalyn knowledge could make her a resource for Arjay's IED experiments, or a warning voice against them.
