# Arjay

Status: `Sessions 1-4 + confirmed visual reference`

## Identity (campaign facts)
- Canonical name: `Arjay` (DM source). Historical variant in older notes: `Arhjay`.
- Species: Half-orc
- Role: Mercenary who frequently travels between Ten-Towns

## Session 1 confirmed facts
- Served as a local connector for Hadrian in Lonelywood.
- Shared rumor that an Arcane Brotherhood wizard was causing a stir in Easthaven.
- Joined the White Moose hunt with Za'mora, Hadrian, and Tar.
- Interrogated a captured awakened badger.
- Learned that Lady Ravasin is conducting ritual killings and attempting to court Auril's favor.
- Learned Ravasin's order is trying to persuade Ten-Towners to follow Auril, and that Auril has allegedly forsaken them.

## Session 3 confirmed facts (2026-03-17)
- Returning to Lonelywood, realized the rescued girl was still alive but barely; reversed course to bring her to Tar.
- During the stair defense, collapsed the staircase roof to seal in the remaining one-eyed psychic creatures.

## Session 4 confirmed facts (2026-04-14)
- Assisted Speaker Nimsy Huddle with stitching a wounded man at the Lonelywood chapel and received the Duergar-pattern intel and the perpetual-dark theory.
- On Maer Dualdon, revealed to the awakened plesiosaurus that Tar killed Lady Ravasin; the plesiosaurus then specifically targeted Tar.
