# Image Continuity Guidelines

Use these rules when generating campaign images:

1. Keep each character's silhouette, coloring, and signature gear consistent with their portrait reference.
2. Preserve the cold Icewind Dale environment cues (snow, wind, fur/leather/metal weathering) unless scene context says otherwise.
3. Favor painterly fantasy illustration style compatible with existing portraits.
4. If a generated image would hide identity (helmets/masks/angle), add at least one clear identity cue (weapon, fur cloak, shield emblem, horns, species traits).
5. If details conflict across sources, prioritize the newest explicitly user-confirmed source.

## Party-wide style cues
- Tonal direction: gritty heroic, survival in severe winter.
- Materials: worn metal, layered cloth, fur, leather, frost and mud wear.
- Lighting: cool ambient light with warm spell/fire accents.
