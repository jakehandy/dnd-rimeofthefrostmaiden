# One-Shot 001 — "To Kill a Wizard: A Rime of the Frostmaiden One-Shot"
## Real date: 2026-03-03 — In-game date: Unknown — Location: Easthaven

> Note: This is a standalone one-shot with its own temporary characters. The events are canon to the world and inform the main campaign's broader context (Arcane Brotherhood, Netherese ruins, parasitic worm creatures).

## Summary
Three Easthaven patriots infiltrate the Town Hall dungeon to interrogate a captured Arcane Brotherhood wizard before his execution, track the rest of his group to a Netherese ruin site using a sentient Bag of Holding, and defeat the remaining wizards — though the bag and one party member are blown away in a blizzard, and two characters shelter in the Netherese tower.

## One-shot characters (temporary)
- **Kodo** — Psi Warrior; Easthaven patriot.
- **Creuse Pelleteuse** — Archaeologist and Netherese enthusiast; Easthaven patriot.
- **Blinky** — Mime; Easthaven patriot; has access to invisibility.

## What happened

### The mission
- **Scython** (recruiter, identity/faction Unknown) enlisted Kodo, Creuse, and Blinky to hunt down and kill the Arcane Brotherhood wizards responsible for killing Easthaveners.
- The Easthaven guard captured one wizard, **Dzaan**, but plan only to execute him publicly — not to pursue the rest of his group.
- Scython suggested breaking into the dungeon to extract information from Dzaan first.

### Town Hall infiltration
- Kodo cut a hole through the Town Hall roof.
- Creuse dug a hole underneath the Town Hall.
- Blinky walked through the front door.
- Two guards were killed in the dungeon. Creuse bit off one guard's penis.
- The group encountered **Johnny Bag'em**, a sentient Bag of Holding, during or after the fight; Bag'em aided them and claimed to have methods of locating the other wizards.

### What Johnny Bag'em revealed
- **Vraxil** (wizard; identity/faction Unknown beyond Arcane Brotherhood affiliation) recruited Dzaan and three other wizards to pillage a Netherese ruin site for a **special artifact**.
- The artifact was found and placed inside Bag'em. Bag'em does not know what the artifact is but knows it is critically important to the wizards.
- A tracker inside Bag'em can be used to locate the other wizards.
- Dzaan claims the wizards did not kill the Easthaveners — he will not elaborate on how they actually died.
- Objects Bag'em spat out seemingly at random: a broom of flying, a shovel, the decaying corpse of a woman.

### En route to the ruin site
- The group followed the tracker toward the other wizards.
- They found **Eli**, a missing Easthavener, on the verge of death.
- Eli reported: something came out of the Netherese ruins after the wizards opened it; all of his friends went mad; he was attacked by a creature but escaped.
- Eli collapsed in abdominal pain — **a flying worm-like creature erupted from his stomach** and attacked the party.
- The party killed the worm creature but took heavy damage.

### Netherese ruin site
- The party reached the ruin site and the wizards' camp.
- Kodo and Blinky overheard wizards say **Vraxil will be furious** when he finds out the bag is missing.
- Blinky turned invisible and wore Bag'em on his front, making Bag'em appear to float — an attempt to spook the wizards.
- The wizards saw their chance to recover the artifact and attacked.
- A blizzard intensified during the fight.
- The party defeated the wizards.
- **The blizzard blew Blinky and Bag'em** (on the broom of flying) far into the tundra — their location is Unknown.
- **Creuse and Kodo sought shelter in the Netherese tower** at the ruin site.

## NPCs and entities
- **Dzaan** — Arcane Brotherhood wizard; executed publicly (burning) in Easthaven. Status: **Dead** (executed).
- **Vraxil** — Arcane Brotherhood wizard; recruited Dzaan's group; not present at the ruin site; status Unknown. Critically important to him: the artifact inside Bag'em.
- **Johnny Bag'em** — Sentient Bag of Holding; contains an unknown Netherese artifact; contains an internal tracker. Current location: Unknown (blown away with Blinky).
- **Scython** — Easthaven figure who hired the one-shot party; faction/identity Unknown.
- **Eli** — Easthavener survivor; infected by a parasitic worm creature; died or near death when the creature erupted from him.
- **Flying worm-like creature** — parasitic entity released from Netherese ruins; erupts from a host's abdomen; drove other hosts mad.

## Locations
- Easthaven Town Hall dungeon
- Road between Easthaven and the Netherese ruin site
- Netherese ruin site and tower (Kodo and Creuse sheltering here)

## Key facts for main campaign context
- The Arcane Brotherhood is actively pillaging Netherese ruins in the region.
- At least one Netherese ruin has been opened and released parasitic worm-like creatures that infect hosts and drive them mad.
- An unknown Netherese artifact of high importance is inside Bag'em somewhere in the tundra.
- Vraxil is at large and will likely seek to recover the bag and artifact.
- The Easthaven Brotherhood rumor from Session 1 (Hadrian directed toward Easthaven) is now contextualized — Dzaan was the wizard causing a stir, now executed.
