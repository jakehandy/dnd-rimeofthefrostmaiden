# One-Shot - "To Kill a Wizard" - 2026-03-03

> Side adventure with a separate cast (not the main party). Important because it intersects the main campaign's Arcane Brotherhood / Netherese-artifact threads and the rising blizzard. The main-party PCs (Tar, Uriel, Arjay, Hadrian, Za'mora) do not appear.

## Cast (one-shot characters)
- **Kodo** — Psi Warrior; Easthaven patriot.
- **Creuse Pelleteuse** — archaeologist / Netherese enthusiast; a Druid Tar knows in Easthaven (see Session 4: Tar wants to bring Cara to her).
- **Blinky** — a mime.
- **Scython** — recruiter who hires the trio.
- **Johnny "Mr." Bag'em** — a sentient Bag of Holding.

## Summary
- Scython recruits the three Easthaven patriots to hunt and kill a band of Arcane Brotherhood wizards blamed for killing some Easthaveners.
- The Easthaven guard captured one wizard, **Dzaan**, but plan only to make an example of him, not pursue the rest. Scython sends the trio to break into the dungeon and interrogate him.
- A sentient Bag of Holding, **Johnny Bag'em**, aids the group and reveals the wizards' true mission.

## What happened
- Kodo cut through the Town Hall roof, Creuse dug under it, and Blinky walked in the front door. They killed two dungeon guards (Creuse bit off one's penis).
- Johnny Bag'em joined the fight and offered to help find the other wizards. He spat out random objects: a broom of flying, a shovel, and the decaying corpse of a woman.
- Bag'em revealed that a wizard named **Vraxil** recruited Dzaan and three other wizards to pillage a Netherese ruin suite for a special artifact, which they found and stored inside Bag'em. He doesn't know what the artifact is, only that it's very important to the wizards.
- Dzaan was desperate to keep the bag and claimed the wizards did NOT kill the Easthaveners — but wouldn't say how they died.
- Using a tracker from inside Bag'em, the group set out and found a dying Easthavener, **Eli**, who said something came out of the Netherese ruins after the wizards opened it, driving his friends mad before attacking him.
- A flying worm-like creature erupted from Eli's stomach and attacked; the party killed it but took heavy damage.
- At the Netherese ruin/wizard camp, Kodo and Blinky overheard that **Vraxil will be furious** when he learns the bag is missing.
- Blinky turned invisible and held Bag'em out front to make it appear to float and spook the wizards. The wizards instead attacked to retrieve the artifact. A blizzard began to pick up.
- The party defeated the wizards, but the blizzard grew so strong it blew Blinky and Bag'em (on the flying broom) far into the tundra. Creuse and Kodo sheltered in the Netherese tower.

## Campaign hooks / connections
- Confirms the Arcane Brotherhood lead (Easthaven) the main party has been hearing about since Session 1.
- A pattern emerges: opening Netherese ruins releases dangerous creatures — echoing the moon-cave black-crystal door (Sessions 2-3) and the "they are awake" warning.
- **Vraxil** is an unresolved Brotherhood threat hunting a Netherese artifact (now inside Johnny Bag'em, lost in the tundra).
- **Creuse Pelleteuse** is later named by Tar as a Druid in Easthaven who might help reverse Cara's ritual (Session 4).
- The intensifying blizzard ties into the campaign's broader everlasting-rime / Auril situation.
