# Session 001 - 2026-01-13

## Summary
- The party converged in and around Lonelywood while following separate leads tied to awakened animals, the White Moose, and Lady Ravasin.
- They located and freed Uriel from a moon-phase ritual site, then defeated the awakened White Moose during a hard fight with a pack of awakened badgers.
- New major threads emerged: Lady Ravasin's ritual murders, Auril's apparent abandonment of Ravasin's order, and a cryptic warning about Ythryn.

## What happened
- Uriel received a vision from Michael: investigate what happened to Auril for the Morninglord, and protect a being on a sacred quest.
- Uriel awoke in a tundra and was dragged by awakened badgers to an unknown ritual location at Lady Ravasin's request.
- Hadrian (Eladrin wizard) arrived in Lonelywood searching for a friend, and local distrust of magic directed him toward Arjay.
- Arjay shared rumors that an Arcane Brotherhood wizard was causing a stir in Easthaven.
- Za'mora (Tabaxi guide) was hired by a frantic mother who said her daughter was kidnapped by the White Moose.
- Tar learned from a snow fox that animals were awakening, the White Moose lived in a moon cave, and a deceptive "lady with the owl face" should be avoided.
- The party tracked the moose to a ritual clearing with moon-phase alignment and corpse sacrifices; Uriel was found alive and freed.
- The awakened White Moose attacked with awakened badgers; the party survived a hard fight and killed the moose.
- A captured badger indicated Lady Ravasin was leading these killings to court Auril's favor and recruit Ten-Towners; Auril has allegedly forsaken them.
- Tar triggered a shriveled elf corpse to speak: "Ythryn is lost. The seal is broken. They are awake."
