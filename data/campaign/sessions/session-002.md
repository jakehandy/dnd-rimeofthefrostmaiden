# Session 002 — Real date: 2026-02-10 — In-game date: Unknown

## Summary
The party entered the moon cave — revealed to be a Netherese building encased in ice — explored its defenses via a silver control gauntlet, encountered and killed Lady Ravisin deep within, and fled after Za'mora opened the warded black crystal door and released something terrible from within.

## What happened

### Approach and setup
- The party made their way to the "moon cave," which turned out to be an ancient Netherese building long encased in ice from disuse.
- Hadrian identified the structure as Netherese in origin and created an alarm at the entrance.
- Za'mora kept watch at the cave mouth. Tar's fox companion refused to enter (frightened) and waited outside.

### Gauntlet discovery and cave exploration
- A silver gauntlet rested in the central chamber. Uriel hastily put it on and began experimenting.
- The gauntlet controls the cave's defense mechanisms.
  - `suppress`: burns Uriel; turns stair glow off.
  - `sentry`: no burn; turns stair glow on (and activates defense statues).
- Party advanced through the cave using the gauntlet to manage defenses, though the gauntlet showed signs of significant strain.
- Tar was healed during exploration: Za'mora applied a healing effect (+9 HP, from 10/24 to 19/24), then Uriel cast Cure Wounds (+15 HP, capped at 24/24 full).
- Tar used a Guiding Bolt (spent one spell slot).
- Table rule confirmed: Mountain Form is used in place of Starry Form in active play.
- Initiative note from the statue encounter: Tar rolled 8, acting fifth in party order.
- The `sentry` switch was re-enabled during descent and triggered two giant statues to activate; combat ensued. An enemy made two attacks into Tar — one missed, one hit for 17 damage (mitigation application Unknown). Uriel used the switch to end the encounter.
- Za'mora discovered elaborate wall carvings depicting magnificent floating cities in the sky with wizards shaping the world below (Netherese imagery).
- The party solved a moon-themed puzzle and opened a door leading further into the structure.

### Lady Ravisin encounter and death
- Deep in the cave the party encountered a woman in long white robes wearing an owl mask.
- She warned them explicitly: **do not open the door with the black crystal inlaid in it — it holds back something terrible.**
- Tar, overcome with grief and anger at the harm Lady Ravisin had caused the animals of the area, attacked her.
- Arjay objected strongly — he wanted information from her about Ythryn.
- **Lady Ravisin was killed by Tar.** Arjay was disgusted.

### The black crystal door and the flight
- Za'mora removed the black crystal from the door.
- Inhuman screams erupted from within; something began trying to break down the door from the other side.
- **The party fled the cave.**
- Arjay took the body of the kidnapped girl (believed dead at this point) and began traveling back to Lonelywood.
- The rest of the party sheltered at the mouth of the cave.
