# Session 003 - 2026-03-17

## Summary
- The party, still inside the Netherese moon-cave structure, defended the top of the stairs against a horde of one-eyed psychic creatures climbing up from below.
- Arjay returned with the kidnapped girl (still unnamed this session) and Tar performed a ritual that stabilized her but could not fully reverse Lady Ravasin's ritual.
- Hadrian identified the black crystal recovered from the moon cave as an ancient Netherese warding artifact.
- The party sealed the stairwell with a collapse to contain the remaining horde.

## What happened
- Trekking back to Lonelywood, Arjay realizes the girl he is carrying is alive but barely; her near-death state is the result of Lady Ravasin's interrupted ritual. Arjay turns back to bring her to Tar.
- Hadrian studies the black crystal from the moon cave and concludes it is an ancient Netherese artifact used for warding; he cannot personally activate it.
- Za'mora hears creatures climbing the stairs of the Netherese structure. Many are killed by the stair protections, but enough survive to threaten the top. Za'mora rallies the party for combat.
- Arjay arrives with the girl. Tar examines her and confirms he can stabilize her via his own ritual, but cannot fully reverse the original ritual's effects.
- As the horde reaches the top of the stairs, Tar and Uriel begin the stabilization ritual on the girl; Hadrian, Arjay, and Za'mora form the front line.
- Hadrian attempts to enter the mind of one of the creatures and is bombarded with psychic energy; he flags the one-eyed monstrosities as "more than they seem."
- Za'mora and Hadrian eliminate most of the escaping horde.
- Arjay collapses the staircase roof, sealing in the remainder of the horde.
- Tar's ritual succeeds. The girl is stabilized but left unconscious on the cave floor. Tar pays a heavy personal energy cost.
