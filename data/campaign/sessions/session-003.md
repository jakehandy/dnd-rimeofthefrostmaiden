# Session 003 — Real date: 2026-03-17 — In-game date: Unknown

## Summary
Arjay discovers the kidnapped girl is barely alive (not dead) and returns her to Tar. While Hadrian studies the black crystal and Za'mora monitors a creature horde ascending the Netherese stairs, the party splits to fight off the emerging one-eyed monstrosities and stabilize the girl. Tar successfully performs a ritual to save her at significant personal cost; Arjay seals the staircase roof to contain the rest of the horde.

## What happened

### Arjay's discovery
- While trudging through the forest back toward Lonelywood, Arjay realized the kidnapped girl he was carrying was **alive — barely.**
- He determined she is the victim of Ravisin's interrupted ritual, left in a **liminal state** on the verge of death — the ritual was cut short when Tar killed Ravisin, preventing its completion.
- Arjay begrudgingly turned back to bring her to Tar.

### At the cave mouth (simultaneously)
- **Hadrian** studied the black crystal removed from the Netherese door last session.
  - Conclusion: it is an **ancient Netherese warding artifact** used for its warding properties.
  - Hadrian cannot make use of it himself.
- **Za'mora** heard the creatures from behind the door attempting to climb the Netherese staircase.
  - Many were dying from the stair's built-in protections.
  - Some were getting through.
  - Za'mora prepared the group for an imminent fight.

### The split effort
- Arjay returned with the girl.
- **Tar and Uriel** focused on the stabilization ritual.
- **Hadrian, Arjay, and Za'mora** held off the creature horde at the top of the stairs.

### The horde fight
- Creatures arrived at the top of the stairs and attacked.
- Hadrian attempted to enter the mind of one creature and was **bombarded with psychic energy** — these entities have strong mental defenses or are inherently psychically dangerous.
- The creatures are described as **one-eyed monstrosities.**
- Za'mora and Hadrian killed most of the escaping creatures.
- **Arjay blasted the roof of the staircase**, collapsing it and sealing the remaining horde below.

### Tar's stabilization ritual
- Tar studied the girl and confirmed he could stabilize her with his own ritual — but **could not fully reverse the effects of Ravisin's original ritual.**
- The ritual was performed while combat raged around them (Uriel assisting).
- **Success: the girl is stabilized**, now lying unconscious. The liminal state persists but she is no longer dying.
- **Cost: Tar sacrificed significant personal energy** to complete the ritual. His resources are depleted.

## NPCs encountered
