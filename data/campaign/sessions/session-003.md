# Session 003 - 2026-03-17

## Summary
- The ritual girl Arjay carried off is discovered to be **still alive** — barely — a victim of Lady Ravasin's interrupted ritual, leaving her in a liminal near-death state.
- Hadrian identifies the black crystal from the moon cave as an ancient Netherese warding artifact (he can't wield it himself).
- The creatures released behind the black-crystal door climb the Netherese structure's stairs; the party fights them off at the top while Tar and Uriel race to save the girl.
- Tar stabilizes the girl at great personal cost; she cannot be fully restored. Arjay seals the stairwell, blocking the remaining horde.

## What happened
- On the trek back toward Lonelywood, Arjay realizes the girl is alive but failing. He determines the cut-short ritual left her on the verge of death, and reluctantly turns back to bring her to Tar.
- Hadrian studies the black crystal and concludes it is an ancient Netherese artifact used for warding; he cannot make use of it.
- Za'mora hears the released creatures climbing the structure's stairs — many die to the stairs' protections, but some are getting close to the top. He readies the party for battle.
- Arjay returns with the girl. Tar examines her: he can stabilize her with his own ritual, but cannot fully reverse the original ritual's effects.
- As the creatures reach the top, Tar and Uriel work to save the girl while Hadrian, Arjay, and Za'mora hold the line.
- Hadrian tries to enter one creature's mind and is hit with psychic backlash — these one-eyed monstrosities are more than they seem.
- Za'mora and Hadrian destroy most of the escaping horde; Arjay blasts the stairwell roof, collapsing it to block the rest.
- Tar succeeds in stabilizing the girl (now unconscious on the cave floor), sacrificing much of his own energy.

## NPCs encountered
