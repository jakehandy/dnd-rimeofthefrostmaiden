# Session 004 - 2026-04-14

## Summary
- The party returned the rescued girl, Cara, to Lonelywood and found the town devastated by an attack the villagers describe as "ghosts."
- Two Frost Druids in Lady Ravasin-style attire are proselytizing for Auril at the only undamaged building, the chapel; they show pointed interest in Cara.
- Hadrian uncovers a Chardalyn fragment and Duergar tracks in the chapel cellar, opening a Duergar/Chardalyn thread.
- Crossing the frozen Maer Dualdon toward Termalaine, the party is attacked by an awakened plesiosaurus (revealed to be Lady Ravasin's "daughter"); Cara manifests power, drives it off, stabilizes Tar, then collapses with a new white streak in her hair.

## What happened
- Party decides their first action is to take the girl back to her mother in Lonelywood.
- Tar warns the girl will eventually die without a full reversal of the ritual; he proposes consulting `Creuse Pelleteuse`, a Druid he knows in Easthaven.
- En route to Lonelywood, the girl wakes and identifies herself as `Cara`.
- The group is ambushed by a small golden dragon; Uriel identifies it as his family pet, `Malty`.
- The party arrives to find Lonelywood ravaged: many dead, most buildings heavily damaged or destroyed; villagers report being attacked by "ghosts."
- The town Speaker and Cara's mother are at the chapel with many wounded; the chapel is conspicuously undamaged.
- Two Frost Druids dressed similarly to Lady Ravasin are preaching to the gathered crowd: the attack reflects lack of faith in Auril; everyone must repent and turn to her.
- Tar confronts the Frost Druids. They show pointed interest in Cara, ask Tar how she came to be in his company, and — in Druidic between themselves — note this appears to be the girl Lady Ravasin was using for her ritual.
- Arjay finds the town Speaker, `Nimsy Huddle`, tending to a wounded man. After assisting with stitching, he learns:
  - The attack matches the nature of recent Duergar attacks elsewhere in the Ten Towns, but is unprecedented in scale.
  - Nimsy fears the Duergar are becoming bolder/more aggressive in the perpetual dark.
  - Some people believe the darkness and everlasting rime themselves are the work of the Duergar.
- Za'mora brings Cara to her mother. The mother is shocked at what has been done to Cara, begs the party to fix her, and offers Za'mora a family heirloom `horn of blasting` as compensation.
- Uriel has a vision in his sleep from Michael, who asks for an update on Auril. When Uriel mentions the creatures released in the Netherese building, Michael appears disturbed.
- Hadrian investigates the town at night and finds a single piece of `Chardalyn` (rare pitch-black mineral) in the chapel cellar; he concludes a large stash was once kept there. Duergar tracks are visible near the cellar entrance.
- Party departs for `Termalaine`, the nearest town, en route to Easthaven.
- Party spots a set of Duergar tracks and decides to try to beat the Duergar to Termalaine by crossing the frozen `Maer Dualdon`.
- On the ice they are attacked by an awakened plesiosaurus. After Arjay reveals that Tar killed her mother, Lady Ravasin, the plesiosaurus targets Tar.
- Cara enters a magical state, drives off the plesiosaurus, stabilizes Tar, and falls unconscious. A white streak forms in her hair.
