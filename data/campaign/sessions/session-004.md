# Session 004 - 2026-04-14

## Summary
- The girl wakes and names herself **Cara**. Tar warns she will die unless the ritual is fully reversed, and proposes taking her to a Druid he knows in Easthaven, **Creuse Pelleteuse**.
- The party returns to **Lonelywood** to find it nearly destroyed — attacked by "ghosts," with heavy casualties and damage; only the chapel stands intact.
- Two Frost Druids (Ravasin's order) proselytize at the chapel and show pointed interest in Cara, recognizing her as Ravasin's ritual subject.
- Evidence mounts that **Duergar** are behind escalating attacks across the Ten-Towns, tied to a **Chardalyn** stash hidden in the chapel cellar.
- The party sets out for **Termalaine** (en route to Easthaven), crossing the frozen **Maer Dualdon**, and is attacked by an awakened plesiosaur — Ravasin's "child" — that nearly kills Tar. Cara erupts in power to drive it off, then collapses; a white streak appears in her hair.

## What happened
- En route to Lonelywood the girl wakes and identifies as **Cara**. Tar notes she will eventually die without a full reversal and suggests the Easthaven Druid **Creuse Pelleteuse**.
- The party is ambushed by a small golden dragon — **Malty**, identified by Uriel as his family pet.
- They reach Lonelywood: ravaged, many dead, most buildings destroyed; villagers say **ghosts** attacked. The undamaged chapel shelters the Speaker, Cara's mother, and the wounded.
- Two **Frost Druids** (similar attire to Ravasin) preach that the attack was punishment for lacking faith in Auril and demand repentance. Tar confronts them.
- The Druids take keen interest in Cara, noting in Druidic that she must be the girl Ravasin used for her ritual.
- Arjay helps Speaker **Nimsy Huddle** stitch a wounded man. She confides the attack resembles other **Duergar** attacks across the Ten-Towns — but at unprecedented scale — and fears the Duergar are growing bolder in the endless dark. Some believe the Duergar are behind the darkness and everlasting rime itself.
- Za'mora brings Cara to her mother, who is horrified to learn what was done. She begs the party to fix Cara and gives Za'mora a family heirloom, a **horn of blasting**, as payment.
- Uriel dreams of **Michael**, who asks for an update on Auril; when Uriel mentions the creatures released in the Netherese building, Michael seems disturbed.
- Hadrian investigates Lonelywood at night and finds a single piece of **Chardalyn** (rare pitch-black mineral) in the chapel cellar — concluding a large stash was once there. **Duergar tracks** were near the cellar entrance.
- The party departs for **Termalaine**, spots Duergar tracks, and decides to cross the frozen **Maer Dualdon** to beat them there.
- An awakened **plesiosaur** attacks, trying to kill Tar after Arjay reveals he killed her mother, **Lady Ravasin**. Cara enters a magical state, drives off the plesiosaur, stabilizes Tar, and collapses unconscious. A **white streak** forms in her hair.
