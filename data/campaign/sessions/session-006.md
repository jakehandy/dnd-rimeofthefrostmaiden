## Summary
- The party meets **Vellyenne Harpell**, a wizard of the **Arcane Brotherhood**, and her Dragonborn companion **Scrimwich** — resolving the Session 5 "mysterious woman + hooded Dragonborn" mystery.
- Vellyenne strikes a deal: recover her stolen orb from fellow Brotherhood wizard **Vraxil** in exchange for funding and 1000 gp, plus a valuable magic item if Vraxil is brought back alive.
- **Masthew** holds a ceremony honoring the party for saving Termalaine; redirected offerings meant for Auril unsettle some townsfolk.
- En route to **Targos**, the party is ambushed by bandits acting on a Frost Druid's orders who wants Cara and is moving more Chardalyn to other towns.

## What happened
- Vellyenne Harpell introduces herself as a wizard of the Arcane Brotherhood, "strangely attractive" per the party's read, accompanied by her Dragonborn companion Scrimwich.
- She's searching for **Vraxil**, another Brotherhood member, who stole a precious family heirloom of hers — a mysterious orb.
- She explains **Chardalyn**: volatile and explosive in its raw form, but capable of storing spells once refined.
- She grows visibly distressed on learning that the party (specifically **Za'mora**, who removed the black crystal) unleashed the horde of monstrosities sealed in the Netherese moon-cave building.
- She examines Za'mora's black crystal and identifies it as the most refined piece of Chardalyn she's ever seen, infused with a warding spell.
- **The deal**: Vellyenne will fund the party's effort and pay **1000 gp** for the safe return of her orb. If the party also brings Vraxil back alive, she'll additionally give them a valuable magic item.
- **Arjay** tests the explosive properties of unrefined Chardalyn and accidentally sets a Termalaine blacksmith's workshop on fire. He uses what he learns to start designing the first in a planned series of Chardalyn-based IEDs.
- **Masthew** holds a ceremony of thanks for the party's role in defending Termalaine. Townsfolk give the party offerings originally meant for Auril — a gesture that upsets some of the townsfolk.
- The party sets out for **Targos** and is ambushed by bandits on the road.
- Arjay interrogates a captured bandit — using the **immovable rod** to nearly strangle him — and recovers a letter reading: *"those mongrels from out of town are becoming a nuisance. Please take care of them and bring us the girl."* Arjay determines the letter was written by a **Frost Druid**, and that the Druids are moving more Chardalyn to other Ten-Towns.
