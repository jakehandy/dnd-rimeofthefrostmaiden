## What happened
- **Meeting Vellyenne & Scrimwich.** The adventurers meet **Vellyenne Harpell**, a strangely alluring wizard of the Arcane Brotherhood, and her Dragonborn companion **Scrimwich** — the pair who disintegrated four Duergar in Session 5.
- **Vraxil and the orb.** Vellyenne is searching for another Brotherhood member, **Vraxil**, who stole a very precious family heirloom: a **mysterious orb**. She teaches the group about **Chardalyn** — volatile and explosive in raw form, **spell-storing** once refined.
- **The Black Crystal identified.** She is visibly distressed to hear that the group (Za'mora) unleashed the horde of monsters from the Netherese dungeon. She identifies the **Black Crystal** Za'mora carries as the **most refined piece of Chardalyn she has ever seen**, infused with a **warding spell**.
- **The bargain.** Vellyenne offers a deal: find and return her orb for **1,000 gp**; if the party also brings **Vraxil to her alive**, she will give them a **valuable magical item**.
- **Arjay's experiments.** Arjay sets a blacksmith's workshop on fire while testing the explosive properties of unrefined Chardalyn, then uses what he learned to build the first of many **improvised explosive devices** he intends to design.
- **Termalaine's thanks.** **Masthew** holds a ceremony of thanks for the party's role in saving Termalaine. Townsfolk give the party the offerings meant for **Auril** — which upsets some of the townsfolk.
- **Ambush on the Targos road.** The party sets out for **Targos** and is ambushed by bandits. After nearly strangling one to death with the **immovable rod**, Arjay finds a **letter** on him reading: *"those mongrels from out of town are becoming a nuisance. Please take care of them and bring us the girl."* Arjay determines the note came from a **frost druid**, and that the druids are bringing more **Chardalyn** to some of the other towns.
