## Summary
- After a skirmish with bandits, the party finds **Cara has been abducted**; the guard **Theythum** is left unconscious.
- The kidnapper is a red-bearded, eyepatched **mercenary** — the last of a hired team. Za'mora and Arjay track him down and recover Cara.
- Under interrogation, the mercenary reveals the team was hired by **Lady Yshma**, a prominent frost druid of the **Church of the Frostmaiden**, to bring Cara to them.
- The party lets him go and travels to **Targos**, where a new Speaker, **Naerth**, is offering human sacrifices to **Auril** — deeply unpopular with the townsfolk.
- Tar learns he may commission custom armor from **Garn the Hammer**, the greatest blacksmith in Ten-Towns, based in **Bryn Shander**.
- Investigating the kobold-infested **Targos mines**, Arjay interrogates a kobold, **Grek**, and learns the kobolds are shielding Targos from a vein of **Chardalyn** deeper in the mines.
- Grek leads them to the kobolds' "Dragon Lord" — revealed as a white, winged kobold with a snake-headed golden staff: **Snowtoe the Coldbold**.

## What happened
- **Cara taken.** After the bandit skirmish, Za'mora realizes Cara is missing. Arjay finds **Theythum** unconscious amid signs of a struggle — Cara was taken. Theythum comes to and describes the attacker: a man with a **red beard and an eyepatch**.
- **The chase.** The party sets off after this last mercenary. During the pursuit Tar, Uriel, and Hadrian get soaked crossing water and have to strip; a round of crude comparison follows. Za'mora and Arjay press on tracking and eventually catch up to Cara and the bandit.
- **Interrogation.** Za'mora binds the mercenary while Arjay interrogates him. He confesses his team was hired by **Lady Yshma**, a prominent frost druid in the Church of the Frostmaiden, to deliver Cara to them. The party lets him go free.
- **On to Targos.** Reunited, the group travels to **Targos**, learning it has had a recent change of Speakership: **Naerth**, the new Speaker, is offering **human sacrifices to Auril**, to the citizens' dismay.
- **Armor lead.** Tar learns he may be able to get custom-made armor from **Garn the Hammer** — reputed the greatest blacksmith in all Ten-Towns — in **Bryn Shander**.
- **The mines.** Kobolds are still driving miners out of the Targos mines; rumor says a **dragon** lairs within. The party investigates. Arjay interrogates a kobold, **Grek**, who reveals the kobolds are trying to **protect the people of Targos from a vein of Chardalyn** found deeper in the mines.
- **The Dragon Lord.** Grek takes the group to the kobolds' Dragon Lord, who reveals himself to be a kobold as well: a **white, winged kobold** bearing a **golden staff shaped like a snake** — **Snowtoe the Coldbold**.
