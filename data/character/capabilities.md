- Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage
- Gain temporary hit points equal to `2 x Druid level`

### Active cost and progression (`Petrification Pressure`)
- Each activation immediately adds `1 Petrification Mark`
- Marks persist until removed through DM-approved methods

### Petrification Mark chart (active)
1. Dexterity score reduced by 1
2. Speed reduced by 5 ft
3. Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws
4. Charisma score reduced by 1
5. Speed reduced by another 5 ft
6. Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) and Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks
7. Maximum Dexterity reduced by 10
8. Begin each long rest with 1 level of exhaustion
9. Risk of permanent petrification; DM permission required to use form again

### Mark removal (DM discretion)
- Complete significant nature-aligned quests
- Long-term ritual purification (days)
- Sacrifice something meaningful
- Direct intervention from a primal entity
