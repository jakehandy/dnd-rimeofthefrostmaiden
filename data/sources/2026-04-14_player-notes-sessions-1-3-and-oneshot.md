## Raw notes (verbatim)

### Session 1, 1/13/26
- Uriel has a vision from Michael tasking him to find out for the Morninglord what has happened to Auril, goddess of the Icewind Dale, as well as guiding and protecting a being while they complete their sacred quest. He awakes in a twilit tundra being dragged by awakened badgers to an unknown location at the request of their Lady Ravisin
- Hadrian, an Eladrin wizard, arrives to the secluded town of Lonelywood, one of the Ten-Towns of the Icewind Dale, looking for his friend. He is scorned by the Ten-Towners when he mentions wizardry and magic
- He is directed to Arjay, a half-orc mercenary who frequently travels between Ten-Towns. He has heard rumors of a wizard from the Arcane Brotherhood causing a stir in Easthaven
- Za'mora, a Tabaxi guide of the Dale, is approached by a frantic woman claiming that her daughter has been kidnapped by the White Moose who has been terrorizing the people of Lonelywood. She hires him to find her daughter, and Za'mora enlists the help of his former client Arjay and new companion Hadrian for this quest
- Tar, a mountain spirit guide and shaman, has heard rumors that animals in the area are being awakened and causing havoc. He speaks with a snow fox about this and learns:
  - Yes, animals are waking up
  - The white moose lives in the moon cave
  - Stay away from the lady with the owl face. She deceives. She coerces. She grants unwanted gifts. She is not an owl
- Za'mora picks up the tracks of the moose, and the group meets Tar and the fox. Tar joins the group when he learns they are also tracking the white moose and plans to confront the lady with the owl face after
- The party arrives at a clearing with a giant sundial of sorts, directing phases of the moon, with differently aged corpses tied to the ground next to corresponding phases of the moon's "age". A teenage boy, Uriel, is the only live prisoner, and he is cut free by Za'mora
- Uriel is insistent on protecting the group. He gets his opportunity when an awakened white moose appears and attacks the group with a pack of awakened badgers for disrupting his mother's ceremony
- After a harsh battle resulting in a near death experience for Tar, the white moose is killed
- Arjay learns from a captured badger that the Lady Ravisin is conducting the ritual and responsible for these deaths. She and her order are trying to court Auril's favor and convincing Ten-Towners to do the same. Auril has forsaken them
- Tar accidentally triggers the shriveled corpse of an elf to "wake" and the corpse repeats the following phrase: "Ythryn is lost. The seal is broken. They are awake"

### Session 2, 2/10/26
- The party makes their way into the "moon cave", which is not actually a cave at all but appears to be an old building of some sort that had been encased in ice after long years of disuse
- Hadrian determines that the place is Netherese in origin. He creates an alarm at the mouth of the cave as Za'mora keeps watch
- A silver gauntlet sits in the middle of the chamber and Uriel rather hastily puts it on and begins to experiment with it. It appears to control the defense mechanisms of the cave
- Tar's fox friend is frightened of the moon cave and elects to stay outside
- The party advances through the cave with the help of the gauntlet, although the gauntlet is taking significant strain from this
- Za'mora discovers elaborate carvings along one of the walls depicting magnificent floating cities in the sky as wizards shape the world below
- The party encounters a woman in long white robes and an owl mask in a chamber deep within the cave. She warns them not to open the door with the black crystal inlaid in it, for it holds back something terrible
- Tar, overcome with emotion for the harm Lady Ravisin has caused the animals in this area, attacks her, against the wishes of Arjay, who wishes to know more about what she knows of Ythryn. She is killed, much to Arjay's disgust
- Za'mora removes the black crystal from the door and inhuman screams are heard from within as something tries to break down the door. The party flees the cave
- Arjay takes the body of the dead girl and begins traveling back to Lonelywood while the rest of the party stays at the mouth of the cave to lick their wounds

### "To Kill a Wizard: A Rime of the Frostmaiden One-Shot", 3/3/26
- Three Easthaven patriots, Kodo the Psi Warrior, Creuse Pelleteuse the archeologist/Netherese enthusiast, and Blinky the mime, are recruited by Scython to hunt down and kill a band of wizards from the Arcane Brotherhood that killed some Easthaveners a few days ago
- Scython says that the Easthaven guard captured one of the wizards, Dzaan, but are only going to make an example of him and do not plan on finding the rest of his group. Scython suggests breaking into the dungeons to find out what he knows so that the rest of his group can also be brought to justice
- Kodo cuts a hole through the roof of the Town Hall, Creuse digs a hole underneath the Town Hall, and Blinky walks through the front door of the Town Hall. They encounter two guards in the dungeon and kill them. Creuse bites off the penis of one of them
- The group gets help from a sentient Bag of Holding named Johnny Bag'em in the fight. He claims to have methods of finding the other wizards in Dzaan's group. He spits out objects seemingly at random, including a broom of flying, a shovel, and the decaying corpse of a woman
- Mr. Bag'em tells the group that a wizard named Vraxil recruited Dzaan and three other wizards to pillage a Netherese ruin site for a special artifact, which they found and put inside Bag'em. He does not know what this artifact is but knows that it is very important to the wizards. Dzaan seems desperate to keep the bag in his possession and claims that the wizards did not kill the Easthaveners in question but won't elaborate on how they died
- The group uses a tracker found inside Bag'em to lead them to the other wizards. On the way, they find one of the missing Easthaveners, Eli, who is on the verge of death. He says that something came out of the Netherese ruins after the wizards opened it and all of his friends went mad. He was attacked by this creature but was able to escape
- Eli doubles over in abdominal pain as a flying worm-like creature erupts from his stomach and attacks the party. They are able to kill it but take heavy damage
- The party makes it to the Netherese ruin site and the wizards camp. Kodo and Blinky overhear the wizards say that Vraxil will be furious when he finds out that bag is missing
- Blinky turns invisible and puts Bag'em on his front, allowing Bag'em to appear like he's floating in an attempt to spook the wizards. The wizards see their opportunity to retrieve the artifact from Bag'em and attack. The blizzard begins to pick up
- The party is able to defeat the wizards, but the blizzard becomes so powerful that it blows Blinky and Bag'em, who are on their flying broom, far away into the tundra. Creuse Pelleteuse and Kodo seek shelter in the Netherese tower

### Session 3, 3/17/26
- Arjay trudges through the forest on the way back to Lonelywood when he discovers the girl he is carrying is actually alive, but barely. He ascertains that she is the victim of a ritual Lady Ravasin was performing but the ritual was cut short, leaving her in a liminal state on the verge of death. He begrudgingly decides to take her back to Tar to see if he can save her
- Hadrian studies the black crystal from the moon cave and decides that it is an ancient Netherese artifact that was used for its warding properties. He isn't able to make use of it himself though
- Za'mora hears the mysterious creatures attempting to climb the stairs of the Netherese structure. Many are dying from the protections on the stairs but some are making their way closer to the top. Za'mora prepares the group for battle
- Arjay returns with the girl. Tar studies her and determines he can stabilize her with a ritual of his own, but he will be unable to reverse the effects of the original ritual completely
- The creatures arrive at the top of the stairs. Tar and Uriel get to work on saving the girl while Hadrian, Arjay, and Za'mora fight off the horde
- Hadrian tries to enter the mind of one and is bombarded with psychic energy. These one-eyed monstrosities are more than they seem
- Za'mora and Hadrian take out most of the escaping horde while Arjay blasts the roof of the staircase, blocking the rest of the horde from escaping
- Tar is successful at stabilizing the girl, who now lies unconscious on the floor of the cave. He sacrifices much of his own energy to do so
