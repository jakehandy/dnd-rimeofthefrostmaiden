## Key extracted facts
- Arjay realizes the girl he is carrying back through the forest is still alive but barely.
- The girl is the victim of one of Lady Ravasin's rituals; the ritual was cut short, leaving her in a liminal near-death state.
- Arjay decides to bring the girl to Tar to attempt to save her.
- Hadrian studies a black crystal recovered from the moon cave and concludes it is an ancient Netherese warding artifact; Hadrian cannot personally activate or use it.
- Za'mora hears mysterious creatures attempting to climb the stairs inside the Netherese structure; many are dying to stair protections but some are getting close to the top. Za'mora prepares the group for battle.
- Arjay returns with the girl. Tar examines her and determines:
  - He can stabilize her with his own ritual.
  - He cannot fully reverse the original ritual's effects.
- As the creatures reach the top of the stairs, Tar and Uriel work on saving the girl while Hadrian, Arjay, and Za'mora hold the line.
- Hadrian attempts to enter the mind of one creature and is bombarded with psychic energy; the one-eyed monstrosities are "more than they seem."
- Za'mora and Hadrian eliminate most of the escaping creatures; Arjay collapses the staircase roof to seal in the remainder.
- Tar successfully stabilizes the girl, who is left unconscious on the cave floor; the stabilization cost Tar much of his own energy.
