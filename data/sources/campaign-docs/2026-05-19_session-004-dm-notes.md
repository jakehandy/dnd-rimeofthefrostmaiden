# Source Notes: 2026-05-19_session-004-dm-notes.md

## What this source is

## Key extracted facts
- Party decides to take the girl back to her mother in Lonelywood.
- Tar warns the girl will eventually die if the ritual is not fully reversed; he proposes taking her to a Druid he knows in Easthaven named `Creuse Pelleteuse`.
- En route to Lonelywood, the girl wakes and identifies herself as `Cara`.
- The group is ambushed on the way by a small golden dragon; Uriel identifies it as his family's pet, `Malty`.
- Lonelywood is found ravaged and nearly destroyed: many dead, most buildings heavily damaged or destroyed. Villagers describe being attacked by "ghosts."
- The town Speaker and Cara's mother are at the chapel with many wounded; the chapel itself is strangely undamaged.
- Two Frost Druids in attire similar to Lady Ravasin are at the chapel proselytizing: claiming the attack reflects lack of faith in Auril and demanding repentance/return to Auril.
- Tar is disgusted and confronts the two Frost Druids.
- The two Druids show pointed interest in Cara and question Tar about how she came to be in his company. In Druidic among themselves, they note that Cara appears to be the girl Lady Ravasin was using for her ritual.
- Town Speaker is `Nimsy Huddle`. Arjay assists her with stitching a wounded man.
- Nimsy confides:
  - This attack resembles other Duergar attacks across the Ten Towns in nature, but is unprecedented in scale.
  - She fears the Duergar are becoming bolder/more aggressive in the perpetual dark.
  - Some people believe the darkness and everlasting rime are the Duergar's own work.
- Za'mora brings Cara to her mother; the mother is shocked at what has been done to her daughter, begs the party to fix Cara, and offers Za'mora a family heirloom `horn of blasting` as compensation.
- Uriel has a dream-vision of Michael asking for an update on Auril. When Uriel mentions the creatures released in Auril's Netherese building, Michael appears disturbed.
- Hadrian investigates Lonelywood at night and finds a single piece of `Chardalyn` (a rare pitch-black mineral) in the cellar of the chapel; concludes a large stash was previously stored there. Duergar tracks are present near the cellar entrance.
- Party sets off for `Termalaine` (nearest town) en route to Easthaven.
- They spot a set of Duergar tracks and decide to try to beat the Duergar to Termalaine by crossing the frozen `Maer Dualdon`.
- On Maer Dualdon, they are attacked by an awakened plesiosaurus that targets Tar after Arjay reveals Tar killed her mother, Lady Ravasin.
- Cara enters a magical state, drives off the plesiosaurus, stabilizes Tar, and collapses unconscious. A white streak forms in her hair.

- Whether `Malty` will travel with or aid the party going forward is unstated.
