Session 7, 6/17/26

* The adventurers meet Vellyenne Harpell, a strangely attractive wizard of the arcane brotherhood, and her Dragonborn companion, Scrimwich.
* Vellyene tells the group she is looking for another member of the brotherhood, Vraxil, who stole a very precious family heirloom, a mysterious orb. She also tells the group about Chardalyn and its volatile properties in raw form and its spell storing properties in its refined form
* She seems very distressed to hear that the group (Za'mora) unleashed the horde of monsters from the Netherese dungeon. She identifies the Black Crystal Za'mora has as the most refined piece of Chardalyn she's ever seen and it is infused with a warding spell
